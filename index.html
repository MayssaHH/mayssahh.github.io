Mayssa Hajj Hassan

I am a graduate student in Computer and Communication Engineering at the American University of Beirut (AUB), where I currently work as a Research Assistant with AUB Frontier AI Fellows (FAF). My interests broadly lie in large language models, particularly reasoning, alignment, AI safety, and agents.

This year, I am deliberately focusing on research and exploration—working across different ideas and directions to better understand the questions I want to pursue long term. I plan to begin a PhD in 2027, and I am open to research projects and collaborations that align with these interests and expose me to new problems, methods, and perspectives. This space is where I write about what I am learning, document ideas I am exploring, and share them with others.

Research & Notes

  • Reasoning Vectors

    Research direction · 2026

    Exploring whether reasoning-related capabilities can be represented as directions in activation, representation, or parameter space, and whether these directions transfer across tasks or training anchors.

  • Post-training and Reasoning

    Research direction · 2026

    Studying how supervised and reinforcement-based post-training alter reasoning behavior, and what those changes reveal about learned capabilities and model representations.

  • How Much Can a Small Amount of High-Quality Supervision Teach a Model?

    Research idea · 2026

    Notes around a question I keep coming back to: how surprisingly small amounts of exceptional supervision might shape reasoning capabilities in smaller language models.
